# Recover your password

## Self-service

From the sign-in screen choose "Forgot my password". A reset link
is sent and it expires after 30 minutes.

## Locked account

After five failed attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. If
the lock persists, write to `it@andescloud.example` from your
registered personal email.

## Requirements for the new password

At least 14 characters and no repeating the last 10 passwords used.
