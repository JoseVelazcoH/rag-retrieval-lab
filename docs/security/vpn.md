# Corporate VPN

## Installation

Download the client from the IT portal and sign in with your
corporate account and second factor. The VPN is mandatory to
access staging, databases and internal tools.

## Common problems

If the connection is rejected, check that your clock is synchronized and
that the second factor is active. If the client connects but does not
resolve internal names, restart the local DNS service. When
the problem persists after restarting, open a ticket with the
client log. In the ticket include your operating system, the
client version, the network you connect from and the time of the
attempt; with that data the networks team answers faster.
When someone says I can't log in to the VPN from a hotel or airport
network, that network often blocks outbound port 443.
