# Expenses and reimbursements

## What is reimbursed

Work trips, meals with clients and approved office supplies.
Always keep the invoice in the company's name.

## Process

Upload the invoice in the expenses portal within 30 days. The
reimbursement takes up to 10 business days to reach your account.
After your lead approves it, finance pays on Fridays.

## Rejections

Any expense without an invoice or without a clear business reason
is rejected.
