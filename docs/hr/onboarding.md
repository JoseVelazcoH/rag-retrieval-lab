# Onboarding new people

## First week

On day one you receive your laptop, your access and a buddy.
If I can't log in to the corporate email on day one, write to IT:
it is usually a second factor activation problem.

## Checklist

Set up the second factor, install the VPN, read the security guide
and make your first practice pull request before Friday.

## 30-day goals

Your lead defines three goals with you for the first month and you
review them together at the end of week four.
