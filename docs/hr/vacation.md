# Vacation policy

## Available days

Each person has 20 business days of vacation per year. From the
fifth year of seniority, two extra days are added per completed year,
up to a maximum of 30 days.

## How to request them

Request them in the people portal at least 15 days in advance.
Your lead approves them and the system deducts the balance. Approval
rarely takes more than two business days.

## Balance and expiry

You can carry over up to 10 days from the previous year. Days above
that cap are lost on March 31.
