# Remote work

## Hybrid scheme

We work remotely three days a week. Tuesdays and Thursdays
are office days for in-person meetings.

## Requirements

A stable connection of at least 30 Mbps, an active VPN to access
internal systems and a suitable workspace. Andes Cloud
provides a monthly internet stipend of 25 USD.

## Working from another country

Requires prior approval from HR for legal and tax reasons.
Maximum 30 days a year.
