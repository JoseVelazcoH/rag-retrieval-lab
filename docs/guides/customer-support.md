# Customer support

## Levels

Level 1 answers frequently asked questions. Level 2 investigates
technical problems with access to logs. Level 3 is engineering.

## Response times

First response within one hour for premium customers and within four
hours for the rest. Sometimes a customer says I can't log in to
my account: always ask for the exact time of the attempt and the email used.

## Closing

A ticket is closed only when the customer confirms the problem
is solved. A short survey is sent afterwards.
