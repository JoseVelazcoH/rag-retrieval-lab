# On-call

## Shifts

On-call lasts one week and rotates among four people. The
handover happens on Mondays at 10:00 with a summary of what happened.

## Responsibilities

Respond to critical alerts within 10 minutes. If you cannot
solve it, escalate to the `#infra-oncall` channel without waiting too long.

## Compensation

Each week of on-call earns one compensatory day off.
